## Context

What problem are we solving? What constraints and forces matter? Keep it to why a decision is
needed, not how it is implemented.

## Decision

What we decided and what we ruled out. State the principle that generalizes beyond this codebase.
Keep file paths, key names, and literal values out of the decision; use concepts. Optional one-line
Y-Statement first:

> In the context of <use case>, facing <concern>, we decided for <option> and against <alternative>,
> to achieve <value>, accepting <trade-off>.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder because of this choice? What trade-offs are we accepting? No
implementation inventory; specifics belong in References if at all. Do not add cross-links to other
ADRs here; add them forward in future ADRs.

## Reversibility

One-way or two-way door: what unpicking this costs, and what would force it.
