# Product

Source of truth: `GLOSSARY.md` for domain vocabulary, `docs/design/` for system, `content/**` for
copy.

A change is done when `Quietly confident` still reads as one impression.

## Users: time-pressed, pattern-sensitive

Why hiring managers, peers, and the `Passer-by` `GLOSSARY.md` decide in seconds and spot templates.
Solve keep skills, experience, and personality legible at a glance; keep log entries self-contained
for cold arrivals.

## Purpose: detail at every level

Why portfolio must prove full-stack capability and a distinct perspective. Solve leave one
impression: someone who cares about detail from code to copy to motion. Success is a visitor who
remembers the site, not just the resume.

## Personality: `Quietly confident`

Why craft does the talking. Solve no boastful copy, no decorative excess. Like a mechanical watch:
nothing extraneous, everything intentional. References are Linear and Vercel for opinionated,
dark-mode-native restraint that respects developers.

## Anti-references: not `Generic`

Why `Generic` reads as generated. Solve avoid gradient hero cards, big-number metrics, icon grids,
scroll-jacking, CRT filters, fake typing, glassmorphism, numbered eyebrows; `docs/design/tokens.md`
allows the one sanctioned gradient (`body::before` in `src/app.css`).

## Principles: `Terminal-native`

Why `Terminal-native` not pastiche. Solve keep terminal as medium for real utility `Command Palette`
and Bloomberg density, not costume. Keep `Dark-first` as a stance, with full light fidelity; the
shipped default follows `prefers-color-scheme` until the visitor chooses a theme. Keep
`Anti-generic` no eyebrow scaffolding, `Detail at every level`.

## Content: gated

Why trust pages need honesty. Solve show the full record, including Dennemeyer details.
Market-number widgets are not shipped yet; if they return they are set dressing, not live data, and
must be disclosed to agents via `llms.txt`. Keep `WCAG 2.1 AA`, `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard
and screen reader support, color never sole carrier.
