I started out as a financial risk analyst at a small finance firm. Small meant no process to hide
behind: if a report needed pulling twice, you wrote something to pull it for you.

Somewhere in there, the little tools keeping the risk models running quietly became the part I
actually looked forward to. That was the tell. So I made it official and switched to building
software. I have never once missed those reports.
