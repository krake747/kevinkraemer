These days I build web software for a global company. Most weeks that's React and TypeScript, with
regular detours into our .NET and Go backends whenever the frontend needs something from them.
Somewhere along the way I also became the person who untangles internal processes when they get in
people's way. I've stopped pretending I don't enjoy that part.

I ship early on purpose. Get an honest scrappy version working end to end, learn what you're
actually building, then go back and make it good. No plan survives the first real user anyway.
