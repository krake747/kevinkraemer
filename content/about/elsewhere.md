When I'm not coding I'm usually on a bike somewhere in Luxembourg, road or gravel depending on how
ambitious the weather looks. Otherwise I'm keeping an eye on markets I don't have to hedge anymore,
or working through the local food scene with friends.

I'm modeling finance domain logic in OCaml, with a bit of Rust on the side. At university,
behavioral finance was my favorite area of finance. It gave me a useful lens for understanding why
people make the choices they do, and those ideas still shape how I think about software and user
experience. Kahneman and Thaler especially: _Thinking, Fast and Slow_ lives on my desk.
