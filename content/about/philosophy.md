Finance still shapes how I think about software. Markets run on imperfect information; prices move
because people keep learning and adjusting. Almost nothing is simply right or wrong, and every
choice trades one thing for another. Product work turns out to be the same game, just with fewer
charts open.

I gravitate toward productivity problems. Software should slot into how people already work, not
fight them. My favorite compliment for a tool is when it gets forgotten: everyone just gets on with
their day, with time back for the parts only they can do.
